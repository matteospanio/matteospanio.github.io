I'm an AI research engineer working on audio, music and multimodal generative models, and a PhD
candidate in Brain, Mind and Computer Science at the University of Padua, based at the
[Centro di Sonologia Computazionale](https://csc.dei.unipd.it/). In 2025 I spent six months as a
visiting PhD student at the Music Technology Group of Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona.

Three threads run through the work: recovering audio cultural heritage from degraded magnetic tape,
as part of the IEEE 3302-2022 standard I help develop at [MPAI](https://mpai.community); teaching
generative models the correspondences between taste and sound; and asking what language models
actually understand about music notation.

Alongside it I maintain [TorchFX](https://matteospanio.github.io/torchfx/), an audio DSP library
that runs on the GPU, and I play clarinet with the
[Orchestra di Padova e del Veneto](https://www.orchestrapadova.it/). The long version is in the
[CV](/cv/).
