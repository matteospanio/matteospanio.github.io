A new paper titled _A novel derivative-based approach for the automatic detection of time-reversed audio in the MPAI/IEEE-CAE ARP international standard_ by Marina Bosi, Fabio Zanini, Alessandro Russo, Sergio Canazza and me has been accepted at the [AES Show 2024](https://aes2.org/events-calendar/aes-show-2024-ny/), which will take place in New York from 8 to 10 October 2024. See you in New York.
