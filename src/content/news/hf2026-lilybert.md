We release Lilybert, a new model for music understanding that brings the LilyPond notation format to deep learning. It is available for download on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/csc-unipd/lilybert), alongside an open dataset published on Zenodo. Learn more about it in our [preprint paper](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.10628).
